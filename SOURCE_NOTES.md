# Sources and model limits

Breakwater Market is a fictional six-day harbor game about shared shocks, insurance limits and cash available for repairs. All prices, probabilities and service timings are invented. This is not financial advice or a representation of a real insurance product.

## Financial concepts

The National Association of Insurance Commissioners explains premiums, covered events and deductibles:
https://content.naic.org/consumer/how-does-insurance-work

The Texas Department of Insurance explains how a deductible reduces a covered claim, including losses smaller than the deductible:
https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/deductibles.html

The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau explains accessible reserves for unexpected expenses:
https://www.consumerfinance.gov/an-essential-guide-to-building-an-emergency-fund/

These sources informed the concepts, not the invented prices. Real policies can include exclusions, waiting periods, different deductibles and other conditions. The game's immediate claim payment and fixed repair prices are simplifications. No real savings target or personalized recommendation is provided.

## Probability and interpretation

One incident can damage several stalls together. Their damage is therefore not independent. Equal average damage does not imply an equal chance of being unable to fund all repairs at once. The preview enumerates the public event tickets and includes existing closures in the repair need. Only new covered damage creates an insurance claim.

A lucky outcome does not establish that a decision was good under the known odds. A zero repair-funding risk does not guarantee the final lantern goal. The automated policies described in VERIFICATION.md use one-day objectives and do not establish an optimal strategy.

## Design context and credits

The prototype was developed for the financial-literacy theme of Zoud GameLab: https://itch.io/jam/zoud-gamelab . Public game descriptions were inspected for context, but competing games were not played or benchmarked. No entrant's source, art or assets are copied.

The game, interface shapes, documentation and tests were developed with OpenAI Codex assistance, including independent design and source review. No third-party art, music, paid API or runtime library is bundled. Browser and Node are execution environments. The game makes no runtime network request and stores no personal data.

## Unmeasured outcomes

No human learning gain, behavior change, entertainment rating, accessibility certification, prize competitiveness or competitor-performance advantage has been measured.
