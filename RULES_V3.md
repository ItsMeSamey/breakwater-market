# Rules v3: sales, operating supplies and available cash

November 6, 2026. Root-authorized single economy revision, accepted after inspecting the retained v2 diagnostic and before any v3 execution. This is a rationale-led development change, not a successful result. Keep the v1/v2 rules, source snapshots, seals and adverse results intact.

## Why this revision exists

All three causal policies met the lantern goal on all 40 v2 seeds. Every game reopened all stalls by the end of every day. Reserve and Margin chose identical plans, and their mean cash exceeded the final cash target by day two. The original economy did not maintain the intended reserve-versus-risk tension. Raising the goal alone would not repair that mechanism.

## One changed rule

Each open stall pays one coin of operating supplies from its own daily gross sales after event settlement. Gross sales remain three coins at front-row locations or two coins at back-row locations, plus one if the stall occupies the day's publicly known hotspot. Thus net income is two/front or one/back, plus the hotspot bonus.

Closed stalls have no sales and no supply expense. The cost is taken from sales, not demanded beforehand. Gross sales always exceed the supply expense, so this rule creates no debt, negative income or new borrowing mechanism. The settlement ledger must show gross sales, supplies and net cash received; the board's displayed earning amounts must clearly identify whether they are gross or net.

## Unchanged rules

All rules in RULES_V2.md and its referenced records remain unchanged except the net-income calculation above: six days, three persistent stalls, six berths, starting cash six, initial locations [0,1,5], two planning actions, move cost one, scheduled repair three plus an action, emergency repair four without a planning-action charge, and the once-per-day all-closed rescue job. The rescue grants four coins for one action and remains usable from zero cash.

Coverage still costs two per day for either surge or fault; aggregate deductible is two and payout cap six. Existing damage does not create a claim. Public forecasts still contain two calm tickets, one named-row surge and one named-column fault. Event, claim and chosen emergency repair still precede sales. The final lantern goal remains all three stalls open with at least 18 coins after day six.

The pure preview still measures the chance that current cash after premium and claim cannot pay all immediate emergency reopening costs, including existing closures. Later sales are unavailable for that settlement. The preview's event/claim/repair model is unchanged; bind its function source to the previously verified version and label any reused verification as retained evidence, not a new execution.

## Bounded evaluation and stop conditions

V3_DIAGNOSTIC_CRITERIA_FREEZE.md is the pre-run interpretation contract. Use the unchanged causal policy logic with independently computed gross-minus-supplies earnings. Report the original DIAG-001..040 development set separately from the fresh QUAL-V3-001..040 qualification set. Preserve all outputs, source pins and partial failures.

This authorizes one bounded revision and diagnostic, not repeated parameter tuning. Persistent universal success, full daily reopening and identical Reserve/Margin plans on the fresh set trigger the declared persistent-triviality conclusion. Universal failure is also adverse. A result avoiding both extremes still does not establish balance, fun, learning or competitive quality; it only supports further concrete rendered play and replay review.
