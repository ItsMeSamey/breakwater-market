How to play
  1. Select a stall, then an empty berth to move it. Moving costs one coin and one action.
  2. Read today's four possible event tickets. A surge hits a whole quay; a cable fault hits a whole lane.
  3. Choose cover, or keep your coins. Cover costs two coins, covers one named incident, and pays the repair bill minus two, up to six coins.
  4. Open the market. Reopen closed stalls for four coins each, then collect income. Closed stalls earn nothing.

You have two planning actions. If every stall is closed, a rescue job gives four coins for one action. Use the other action to reopen one stall. There is no timer and no borrowing.
