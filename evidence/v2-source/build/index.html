How to play
  1. Select a stall, then an empty berth to move it. Moving costs one coin and one action.
  2. Read today's four possible event tickets. A surge hits a whole quay; a cable fault hits a whole lane.
  3. Choose cover, or keep your coins. Cover costs two coins, covers one named incident, and pays new covered damage minus two, up to six coins. Existing closures do not create a claim.
  4. Open the market. After the incident, emergency reopening costs four coins per stall with no action limit. Then collect income. Closed stalls earn nothing.

You have two planning actions. Scheduled reopening costs three coins and one action, but the stall can be hit again in today's incident. Emergency reopening costs four coins after the incident. These are fictional service prices.

If every stall is closed, a rescue job gives four coins for one action, at most once per day. Use a remaining action to reopen one stall. There is no timer and no borrowing.
