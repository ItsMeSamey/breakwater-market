## Possible next work

The following are planning estimates only. Participation arrangements, external review costs and any funding request would be settled in a separate agreement.

- Human playtesting and onboarding review: arrange a small consent-based session, record task completion and points of confusion, and revise the instructions from those observations. Estimated implementation/analysis effort: 8 to 16 hours. Recruitment method, participation and compensation remain to be arranged.
- Accessibility review with assistive-technology users: test screen-reader announcements, focus order and narrow layouts. Estimated preparation/fix effort: 6 to 12 hours. Specialist participation and compensation would need separate agreement.

- Language adaptation: obtain fluent review of translated game text and financial examples before adding a language. Scope, hours and price depend on the reviewer and language.
